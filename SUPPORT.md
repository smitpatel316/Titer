# Support

Titer is free and open-source software, maintained alongside other work.
Support is best-effort and community-run: there is no guaranteed response
time and no service-level agreement.

## Getting help

- **Ask a question or report a bug:** open an issue at
  <https://github.com/smitpatel316/Titer/issues> — say what you did, what
  you expected, and what happened, and include your Titer version and
  operating system.
- **User guide:** Help → Titer User Guide in the app (F1), or the README at
  <https://github.com/smitpatel316/Titer>.
- **Feature requests:** open an issue and say what you are trying to do —
  the use case matters more than the proposed solution.

## What to expect

- Issues are triaged as time allows. A bug with a small project file that
  reproduces it gets fixed fastest.
- **Security problems:** open an issue with "Security" in the title and
  keep the details minimal until a maintainer replies — do not post
  exploit details in public.
- No paid support is offered. Titer will always be free; if you want to
  help keep it that way, Help → Buy Me a Coffee in the app.
